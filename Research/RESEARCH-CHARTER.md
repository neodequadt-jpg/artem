# ARTEM Research Charter

Status: Active

---

## Mission

ARTEM exists to discover, validate and formalize reproducible engineering knowledge.

The Foundation is not the source of knowledge.

Research is the source of knowledge.

The Foundation preserves validated knowledge.

## Core Principle

Research precedes formalization.

Evidence precedes acceptance.

Reproducibility precedes authority.
