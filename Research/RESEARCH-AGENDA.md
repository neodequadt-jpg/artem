# Research Rule

Research questions are selected before conclusions.

Evidence is collected before claims.

Foundation is updated only after successful validation.

---

# Success Criteria

A research topic is considered complete when:

- existing knowledge has been reviewed;
- competing approaches have been compared;
- weaknesses have been documented;
- a reproducible model has been proposed;
- independent criticism has been addressed;
- an RFC has been accepted.
