# ARTEM Research Doctrine

Status: Active

---

## Purpose

The purpose of ARTEM Research is to discover engineering knowledge rather than invent it.

Research shall precede formalization.

Evidence shall precede acceptance.

Independent validation shall precede Foundation inclusion.

---

## Scientific Integrity

ARTEM does not seek confirmation.

ARTEM seeks truth.

Every research result shall remain open to criticism.

Negative results are valuable.

Failed hypotheses are valuable.

Contradictory evidence is valuable.

---

## Research Principle

The objective of research is not to prove ARTEM correct.

The objective of research is to discover whether ARTEM is correct.

---

---

## Final Rule

If evidence contradicts ARTEM,

ARTEM shall change.

Evidence shall not.
