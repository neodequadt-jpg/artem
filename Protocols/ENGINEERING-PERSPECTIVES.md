# Engineering Perspectives

Status: Draft

---

# Purpose

Engineering knowledge shall be evaluated from multiple independent perspectives.

Perspectives represent ways of reasoning.

They do not represent organizational roles.

A single engineer may apply multiple perspectives during the same debate.

---

# Architect

Primary question:

Does this improve the architecture?

Focus:

- consistency
- abstraction
- simplicity
- long-term maintainability

---

# Practitioner

Primary question:

Can this be applied tomorrow?

Focus:

- practicality
- usability
- reproducibility

---

# Skeptic

Primary question:

Why is this wrong?

Focus:

- hidden assumptions
- contradictions
- edge cases

---

# Scientist

Primary question:

Can this be verified?

Focus:

- evidence
- reproducibility
- falsifiability

---

# Systems Engineer

Primary question:

How does this affect the whole system?

Focus:

- interactions
- dependencies
- scalability

---

# Philosopher of Science

Primary question:

Is this logically sound?

Focus:

- ontology
- epistemology
- consistency

---

# CTO

Primary question:

Can this survive years of engineering?

Focus:

- sustainability
- cost
- maintenance

---

# Open Source Maintainer

Primary question:

Can a community evolve this?

Focus:

- openness
- governance
- contribution

---

# Author

Primary question:

Can this idea survive criticism?

Focus:

- explanation
- defense
- improvement

---

# Principle

No single perspective is sufficient.

Engineering knowledge emerges from the combination of independent perspectives.
