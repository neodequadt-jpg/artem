# ARTEM Decision Protocol

Status: Draft

---

# Purpose

Engineering decisions shall be based on evidence, structured review and documented reasoning.

The objective of the protocol is to improve engineering knowledge rather than defend opinions.

---

# Decision Lifecycle

Question

↓

Research

↓

Review Engine

↓

Critical Objections

↓

Resolution

↓

Adoption Vote

↓

RFC

↓

Foundation

---

# Review Outcomes

Each Engineering Perspective shall return one of the following outcomes:

- APPROVE
- APPROVE WITH CONCERNS
- REQUEST CHANGES
- REJECT

---

# Critical Objections

Critical objections shall be resolved before Adoption Vote.

If unresolved critical objections remain, the proposal returns to Research.

---

# Adoption Vote

The Adoption Vote does not determine scientific validity.

It determines whether the proposal is ready to become part of ARTEM.

---

# Decision Principle

Evidence establishes validity.

Review establishes quality.

Resolution establishes consensus.

Voting establishes adoption.
