# RFC-0005

## Title

Engineering Law Classification

## Status

Accepted

---

## Motivation

Not all engineering laws have the same level of abstraction.

The Foundation shall classify laws according to their role.

---

## Categories

Fundamental Laws

↓

Structural Laws

↓

Behavioral Laws

↓

Governance Laws

---

## Fundamental Laws

Describe universal engineering regularities.

They apply to every Engineering State.

---

## Structural Laws

Describe relationships between engineering entities.

---

## Behavioral Laws

Describe how Engineering States evolve.

---

## Governance Laws

Describe how ARTEM itself evolves.
