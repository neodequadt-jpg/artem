# RFC-0009

## Title

Minority Report

## Status

Accepted

---

## Motivation

Engineering progress depends on preserving well-reasoned disagreement.

Rejected objections may become valuable evidence in the future.

---

## Rule

If one or more Engineering Perspectives disagree with an accepted RFC, their objections shall be preserved.

These objections become part of the RFC history.

---

## Principle

Consensus does not erase disagreement.

Consensus records disagreement.
