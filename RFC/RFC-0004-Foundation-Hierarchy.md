# RFC-0004

## Title

Foundation Hierarchy

## Status

Accepted

---

## Motivation

The Foundation shall distinguish between different categories of engineering knowledge.

Definitions, axioms and laws serve different purposes and must not be mixed.

---

## Hierarchy

Definitions

↓

Axioms

↓

Laws

↓

Corollaries

↓

Practices

---

## Definitions

Definitions introduce terminology.

They describe concepts.

Definitions are neither true nor false.

---

## Axioms

Axioms define the minimal assumptions accepted by the Pre-Theory.

Everything else should be derived from them.

---

## Laws

Laws describe engineering regularities supported by reproducible evidence.

Laws may evolve as engineering evidence accumulates.

---

## Corollaries

Corollaries are logical consequences derived from Laws.

---

## Practices

Practices are recommended engineering techniques.

Practices are not part of the Foundation.
