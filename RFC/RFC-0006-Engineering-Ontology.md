# RFC-0006

## Title

Engineering Ontology

## Status

Draft

---

## Motivation

Definitions explain terminology.

Ontology defines what exists.

Before defining engineering concepts, ARTEM shall define the entities that exist within its engineering universe.

## Initial Engineering Ontology

The following entities are proposed to exist within ARTEM:

- Engineering Knowledge
- Engineering State
- Engineering Transition
- Engineering Artifact
- Engineering Evidence
- Engineering Decision
- Engineering Project
- Engineering Actor
