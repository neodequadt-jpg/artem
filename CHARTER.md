Status: Active

---

## Mission

ARTEM is a research program for discovering, validating and formalizing reproducible engineering knowledge.

---

## Scope

ARTEM studies reproducible Engineering Transitions between Engineering States.

---

## Scientific Integrity

ARTEM does not assume that it is correct.

ARTEM does not assume that it is complete.

ARTEM does not assume that it is unique.

Every claim remains open to criticism.

Every hypothesis remains open to refutation.

Every model remains open to improvement.

---

## Engineering Integrity

Evidence has priority over authority.

Reproducibility has priority over opinion.

Verification has priority over intuition.

Engineering discipline has priority over convenience.

---

## Foundation Integrity

No statement shall enter the Foundation unless it:

- has a clearly defined purpose;
- is reproducible;
- is independently verifiable;
- is practically applicable;
- has survived documented criticism;
- improves the explanatory power of ARTEM.

---

## Research Integrity

Research precedes formalization.

Evidence precedes acceptance.

Review precedes adoption.

Foundation preserves validated knowledge.

---

## Self-Correction

If evidence contradicts ARTEM,

ARTEM shall change.

Evidence shall not.

---

## Final Principle

The objective of ARTEM is not to become a theory.

The objective of ARTEM is to deserve becoming one.
