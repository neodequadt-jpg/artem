# Preamble

Engineering is not the process of writing code.

Engineering is the process of creating reliable knowledge that transforms systems in a predictable and reproducible way.

Software, documentation, architecture, tests, specifications and decisions are engineering artifacts.

They are not the goal.

They are evidence of engineering work.

ARTEM exists to make engineering independent of memory, individuals and artificial intelligence.

Its purpose is to ensure that every engineering transition is reproducible, verifiable and explainable.

Knowledge must outlive its creators.

Engineering must outlive its tools.

Technology will evolve.

Programming languages will evolve.

Artificial intelligence will evolve.

The principles of reproducible engineering must remain.
