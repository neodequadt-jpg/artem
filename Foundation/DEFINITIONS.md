# Definitions

## Engineering

Engineering is the discipline of producing reproducible, verifiable and valuable transitions between Engineering States.

---

## Engineering State

An Engineering State is the complete reproducible description of a project's engineering condition at a specific point in time.

---

## Engineering Transition

An Engineering Transition is a reproducible transformation from one verified Engineering State to another.

---

## Engineering Artifact

An Engineering Artifact is any reproducible evidence produced during an Engineering Transition.

Examples include software, documentation, architecture, tests, specifications, configurations and decisions.

---

## Engineering Evidence

Engineering Evidence is objective information demonstrating that an Engineering Transition has occurred and can be independently reproduced.

---

## Reproducibility

Reproducibility is the ability of independent engineers to obtain equivalent Engineering States by applying the same Engineering Transition under comparable conditions.
