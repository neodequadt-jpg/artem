Status: Draft

Revision: 1

# Purpose

The Engineering Ontology defines the entities that exist within the ARTEM engineering universe.

Ontology answers one question:

"What exists?"

Definitions describe entities.

Ontology establishes their existence.

---

# Fundamental Entities

## Engineering Knowledge

The set of engineering information that may be created, verified, transferred and evolved.

---

## Engineering State

A complete reproducible representation of a project's engineering condition.

---

## Engineering Transition

A transformation from one Engineering State to another.

---

## Engineering Artifact

A persistent engineering object created or modified during an Engineering Transition.

---

## Engineering Evidence

Objective evidence demonstrating that an Engineering Transition occurred.

---

## Engineering Decision

A recorded engineering choice that influences future Engineering States.

---

## Engineering Project

A bounded engineering system that evolves through Engineering Transitions.

---

## Engineering Actor

Any human or artificial agent capable of performing Engineering Transitions.

---

# Principle

Only entities defined by the Engineering Ontology may become part of the Foundation.
