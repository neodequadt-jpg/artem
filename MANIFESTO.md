# ARTEM Manifesto

ARTEM is a Pre-Theory of Reproducible Engineering.

Its purpose is not to defend engineering opinions.

Its purpose is to discover engineering regularities.

ARTEM studies reproducible Engineering Transitions between Engineering States.

Every concept, law and principle shall remain open to independent criticism and validation.

Engineering knowledge is earned through reproducibility.

Engineering theories are earned through evidence.

Until sufficient evidence exists, ARTEM remains a Pre-Theory.

The objective of ARTEM is not certainty.

The objective of ARTEM is continuous improvement through engineering evidence.
