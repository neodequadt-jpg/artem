# ADR-0005

## Title

Governance Freeze

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

The governance model has reached sufficient maturity for Foundation Revision 1.

Continued changes risk destabilizing the engineering process.

---

## Decision

Governance is frozen for Foundation Revision 1.

Changes require:

- Research
- RFC
- Review Engine
- Adoption Vote

---

## Consequences

Future effort shall focus on research and validation rather than governance redesign.
