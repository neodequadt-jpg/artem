# ADR-0004

## Title

Repository Architecture Freeze

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

The repository architecture has reached sufficient maturity to support the development of ARTEM Pre-Theory.

Further structural changes may introduce unnecessary instability.

---

## Decision

The repository architecture is frozen for Foundation Revision 1.

Only document contents may evolve.

Repository structure may change only through a new accepted RFC demonstrating a fundamental architectural deficiency.

---

## Consequences

Engineering effort shifts from repository design to knowledge development.

Foundation Revision 1 becomes the primary engineering objective.
