# ARTEM Debate Protocol

Status: Draft

---

# Purpose

Engineering debates exist to improve engineering knowledge.

They do not exist to defend opinions.

---

# Objective

The objective of every debate is to discover weaknesses.

Agreement is optional.

Improvement is mandatory.

---

# Participants

Every Foundation RFC shall be reviewed by independent roles.

Recommended roles:

- Architect
- Practitioner
- Skeptic
- Scientist
- Systems Engineer
- Philosopher of Science
- CTO
- Open Source Maintainer
- Author

---

# Rules

1. Criticism targets ideas, never people.

2. Every argument shall contain engineering reasoning.

3. Unsupported opinions have no authority.

4. Evidence has priority over intuition.

5. Consensus is not voting.

Consensus is achieved when no unresolved engineering objections remain.

---

# Outcomes

A debate may produce one of the following outcomes:

- Accepted
- Accepted with revisions
- Returned for redesign
- Rejected

---

# Principle

The purpose of debate is not to prove that ARTEM is correct.

The purpose of debate is to make ARTEM more difficult to disprove.
